    Cheats disable trophies and saving — never enable them on your platinum save. There is no mission replay and no New Game+. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave).

    100% Ledger — the road to A Long Road

    The 100% checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion) unlocks after the story, but progress during the story counts. It is closer to 60% true completion: most lines only need part of a set. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests need everything. Trophy targets and 100% targets are often different — the table shows both.

        How to check our work

        • Open any trophy's guide: each step carries its source shortcode ([PPX], [KG], [SNAP], [TGW], [SPOT]).
        • Open app/data/trophies.json in this repository: every trophy, its guide and its step sources are there in plain text.
        • Where a method is unconfirmed, the guide says so on the trophy itself (Bad Luck, I Guess and The Truth Is Out There).
